About the pictures

The engravings are Gustave Doré's, made for the 1861 French edition of the Inferno. The whole-Hell maps are by Sandro Botticelli, about 1485, and by Michelangelo Caetani, Duke of Sermoneta, 1855. Every one of them is in the public domain, and each is credited where it appears. The files are hosted here rather than linked, so the map works when the classroom wifi does not.

The funnel itself is drawn to the plan Caetani used: Jerusalem above, the circles narrowing, Cocytus frozen at the center of the earth.

Cantos and canto numbers follow the Esolen translation, Modern Library. Reading dates come from our own Scope and Sequence.
