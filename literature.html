The Map of Hell

Inferno Drill

Practice for the quiz on Cantos I to XIII

It asks what the quiz asks: for each place in Hell, the sin, the contrapasso, the souls Dante names, the guardian, and the cantos. A wrong answer comes back until you have it, and tells you which canto to reread. It uses the same facts as the Map of Hell and nothing more. Opens in a new tab.
