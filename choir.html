How to practice

Find the piece below. Press Listen next to your part, and open the Score so you can follow your line while it plays. Listen once through, then sing along. Work in small sections and repeat them. Full Choir lets you hear how your part fits with everyone else, and Accompaniment is there when you are ready to sing your part with no help. The 1× button slows a recording down without changing the pitch.
